03 / THE INFORMATION TRAIL

Use a simple process before you trust or pass information to someone else.

STOP
TRACE
COMPARE
DECIDE

STOP

Pause before reacting. Ask if the post is trying to make you angry, scared, or excited.

TRACE

Find the original source. Who created it? When was it published?

COMPARE

Look for the same information from reliable and independent sources.

DECIDE

Then decide whether to believe, comment, save, or share it.

04 / MEDIA INFLUENCE

Media can help us, connect us, teach us, and sometimes pressure us.

“Popularity is not the same as credibility.”

A post can have thousands of views and still contain incomplete or incorrect information.

05 / CREATE RESPONSIBLY

Media literacy is also about how we behave when we create and share.

Respect other people's privacy
Give credit to creators
Check information before sharing
Think about possible impact
Use respectful language
Protect your digital footprint
